Our Grozziie Dot Matrix Printer is a reliable and efficient dot matrix printer designed to meet your business and printing needs. This robust and time-tested printing solution offers exceptional durability. It has advanced printing technology producing crisp and clear dot matrix prints for a wide range of applications. This uses dot matrix technology, which ensures every character is precisely formed, making it ideal for tasks like invoices, receipts, invoices, forms, and more. It is very easy to use. You just need to connect the printer to your device then use app or using any of these interfaces (USB, or Bluetooth), and you are ready to print.

DEVICE COMPATIBILITY: Our Grozziie Dot Matrix Printer is equipped with USB and Bluetooth interfaces. It can support multiple devices for seamless integration. You don’t have to worry about device compatibility for printing your tax invoice, shipping bill, QR code, notes, and so on. Whether it’s your phone or PC this printer will give you a wide range of device compatibility.

APP SUPPORTED: This dot printer comes with easy-to-operate app. Using our app, you can customize your unique design with creativity. You will find a lot of free templates, icons, supporting barcodes, QR codes, insert image options, tables, and so on. Apart from that, you can get support from our customer support team through the app.

FAST PRINT SPEED: This printer can print up to 180 DPI high-density resolution. Which ensures every bit of character is precisely formed, clear, and sharp. The printing speed of our dot printer is 153 mm/s. With a strong printing head and high penetration capability, this printer can print up to 1+5 carbon paper copies.

WIDE RANGE OF USAGE: The Grozziie Dot Matrix Printer is used widely in organization, office & home supplies stickers, storage, notes, file names, study notes, small business and more. Use this thermal label printer to print shipping labels, warehouse labels, waybills, market labels, and so on, helping increase your productivity.

DURABLE BUILD QUALITY: Our dot printer is made of high-quality material, which makes it durable enough to survive any day-to-day use. You can fit this printer at any table in supermarkets, clothing, jewelry stores, small business owners, offices, etc.
