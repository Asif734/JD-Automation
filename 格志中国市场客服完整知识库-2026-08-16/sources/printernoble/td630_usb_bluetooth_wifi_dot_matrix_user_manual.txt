• Regular Cleaning: Clean the printer every three months or after 300 operating hours.  
• Cleaning Tools: Use a dry cloth (for metal parts, use a soft cloth).

• Cleaning the Paper Feed Platform: Remove paper scraps, dirt, and dust.
